# About Me

## Professional background

I am an IT Service Management (ITSM) leader and consultant with 30 years of experience across technical operations, service leadership, governance and transformation. My career has spanned defence, public services, financial services and other regulated environments. Across these settings, my work has centred on making technology services reliable, accountable and responsive to organisational needs.

My early career provided a practical foundation in computing and operational support. Beginning with AS/400 systems management at IBM, I progressed through helpdesk management, team leadership and customer support roles at GE Capital IT Solutions, Datalect and Qanat Software. These experiences developed my understanding of the relationship between technical performance, effective support and the experience of people who depend on technology.

A significant stage in my career was building and leading an IT service management function at London Fire Brigade. Supporting approximately 7,500 staff across 113 sites, including services associated with emergency dispatch, required attention to operational reliability and the consequences of service disruption. I developed the operating model, processes, tooling and team structure, while supporting staff development across service desk, technical support and process roles. The service desk received the Service Desk Institute’s Service Desk Week “Top Service Desk” award. My responsibilities also included supplier relationships, service reporting and stakeholder engagement, alongside bringing several organisational service desks together through a single point of entry.

Subsequent roles in financial services and defence strengthened my focus on governance, information quality and control. At ING / Barclays Direct, I worked on service improvement, software asset management and ITSM tooling, including licence management and the design of supporting data structures and reporting. At Sopra Steria, I managed service asset and configuration processes covering more than 30,000 assets across different technologies, networks and security classifications. These roles demonstrated the importance of dependable service information in supporting operational decisions, compliance and audit readiness.

During more than ten years at Fujitsu, my responsibilities broadened into service architecture, organisational capability and the design and transition of complex services. I led a 30-person service architecture function and supported major bids, transitions and service changes. This involved connecting service management requirements with governance, resourcing, costing, tooling and operational readiness. Developing reusable service design packs and governance models helped improve consistency and reduced the effort required to produce supporting bid material.

I now work through FlowSM Ltd as Director and Principal ITSM Consultant, supporting service management transformation and improvement. My work includes developing service operating models, information structures, governance arrangements and practical approaches to service ownership. Working with product, delivery and operational teams, I translate service management requirements into processes and controls that can be applied in day-to-day operations.

## Professional interests and development

My professional interests include the relationship between service governance, organisational structure and operational delivery. I am particularly interested in how clear ownership, reliable information and proportionate controls support better decisions and service outcomes. Supplier integration, service performance and the effective use of management platforms are recurring themes in my work.

Professional development has accompanied this progression from technical operations into leadership and consultancy. My qualifications include ITIL Version 5 Master, ITIL 4 Master and ITIL v3 Expert, alongside ISO/IEC 20000 Practitioner and Service Integration and Management qualifications. Further study in Agile service management, PRINCE2, AWS and Microsoft Azure has broadened my understanding of the delivery and technology environments in which service management operates.

I also contribute to professional communities as a PeopleCert ITIL Ambassador and through membership of itSMF, the XLA Institute and the Value Stream Consortium. Leadership, mentoring and capability development have remained important throughout my career, complementing my own continuing learning.

## Academic development

I am studying for an MSc in Information Technology Management at the University of Essex Online. The programme provides an opportunity to examine my professional experience through academic research, test established assumptions and broaden my understanding of computing beyond my existing specialisms.

My development priorities include strengthening critical analysis, evaluating research and connecting technical decisions with their business, ethical and social implications. An important part of this process is distinguishing approaches supported by evidence from those that may have become familiar through experience.

This ePortfolio records that development through practical work, academic enquiry, feedback and reflection. It will include work in progress, difficulties encountered and changes made in response to feedback, alongside completed artefacts. Together, these will provide evidence of how my understanding and professional judgement develop throughout the programme.
