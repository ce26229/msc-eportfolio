## Three distinct elements

### 1. Your independently researched response

Develop your own response using academic and reputable industry sources. Cover the technical overview, real-world enterprise applications, ethical and security concerns, and regulatory/governance frameworks. Use case studies to analyse challenges and proposed solutions.

Preserve research notes, source evaluations and drafts as evidence of your own reasoning. This section must not be presented as independently human-authored if AI generated it.

### 2. Clearly labelled Generative AI response

Give a Generative AI tool the same presentation question and summarise its output. Direct excerpts are permitted by the brief when clearly identified as AI-generated.

Keep a comparison record:

| Complete response | To retain |

| Checks of claims and references | To document |

### 3. Critical comparison

Evaluate your research-based response and the AI response against:

- Accuracy, reliability and verifiability of sources.
- Depth, nuance and limitations of analysis.
- Bias, transparency, fabricated claims or references, and other ethical concerns.
- Professional appropriateness and usefulness in computing work.

Support the comparison with specific examples from both responses. Explain how AI can support or hinder professional practice rather than assuming either response is superior.
