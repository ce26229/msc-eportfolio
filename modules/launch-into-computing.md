## 2. Learning outcomes and evidence

The following learning outcomes are reproduced as supplied from the module materials. LO1–LO4 are local labels used to organise evidence in this portfolio.

- **LO1:** Identify and critically analyse computing challenges and processes in business systems, accounting for the current enterprise landscape.
- **LO2:** Gather and synthesise information from multiple sources (including internet resources and business publications) to aid in the systematic design and analysis of computing challenges.
- **LO3:** Critically evaluate appropriate methodologies, tools and techniques to mitigate and/or solve computing issues and their business impact.
- **LO4:** Articulate the legal, social, ethical, and professional issues faced by computing professionals.
